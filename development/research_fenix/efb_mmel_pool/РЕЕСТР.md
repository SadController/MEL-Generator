Source: FAA A320 MMEL Rev32 dated 30.07.2025. Initial observation EFB: A321 IAE WTF, Fenix 2.4.0.4720, 13.09.2026. General applicability is the app's accepted assumption, not the FAA's output. Unsealed flight options are excluded: CPC1 and CPC2 are available separately, a combination is excluded.
