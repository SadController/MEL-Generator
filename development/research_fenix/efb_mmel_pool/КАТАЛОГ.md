# Final catalogue - 53 failures

Single pool for Fenix A319/A320/A321 and CFM/IAE by user decision. There are no categories or filters for the aircraft/engine. This is an assumption of the application, not a change in the applicability of the FAA MMEL. Dispatch relief options requiring unpressurized flight are excluded. CPC1 and CPC2 are stored separately; they are not generated together.

Source: FAA A320 MMEL Rev32 dated 30.07.2025. Names and paths taken from EFB Fenix 2.4.0.4720, image A321 IAE WTF from 13.09.2026. Behavior is untested.

Each record retains the exact EFB path, the MMEL point and pages, and the single opt-out conditions. For combinations, alternative lines from[rules](ПРАВИЛА_СОЧЕТАНИЙ.md)They can replace the terms of the single card. The symbols (M)/(O) and the full text are in the original MMEL; the short card does not reproduce the procedures themselves.
