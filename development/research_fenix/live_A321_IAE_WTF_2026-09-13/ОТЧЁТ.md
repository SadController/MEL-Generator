# What can not be solved even after viewing the menu

1. **A321 and fuel system.** The current A321 menu includes `Fuel pump center → 1/2`, `Left/Right Transfer 1/2`, `Fuel leak → Left/Right Outer`, outer-tank temperature. The center pump card is titled `Fuel pump center 1 configuration` and does not explain model applicability. These points cannot be automatically declared as outer-to-inner A321 valves. They could be generic or reused names; this is a hypothesis, behavior untested. FAA 28-21-02(1) and 28-15-01 on the A321 by analogy do not carry. The A321 requires a precise transfer valves branch and the actual operation of the button.
2. **TAT STBY exists in the current menu.** This is not just an old PDF error. The `ATA 30 → TAT Heat → Stby` button opens the `TAT HEAT STBY CONFIGURATION` card. But the controversy with the two TAT heaters in the FAA 30-31-05 is not over. Do not include as a third applicable heater.
3. **PTU and Blue pump.** The menu contains a common `PTU Fault` and `Elec Hyd Pump Blue Failure`. In the full menu, individual names of failures only for their automatic control are not found. Cards give activation switches, without technical description. Therefore, the previous deviations on FAA 29-23-01 and 29-10-02 remain.
4. **FAC/FCDC/FWC/SDAC/LGCIU #1.** Buttons of these blocks exist, but this does not extend the FAA permissions to the corresponding #2 blocks.
5. **ACT.** Individual items with an explicit ACT name are not found in the menu viewed. The ACT number/location in the screens examined is not explicitly indicated. Do not conclude that there is no ACT only by the capacity or absence of a word in the menu.
6. **Weather radar.** There is no separate Weather Radar/WXR button in this full menu. This is not to say that the radar itself is not implemented.
