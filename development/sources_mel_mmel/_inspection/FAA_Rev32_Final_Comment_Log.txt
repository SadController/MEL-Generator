Installed Physical Secondary Barrier 
(IPSB) MMEL relief needs to be added 
to support required installations for 
new aircraft starting in August 25, 
2025.  Reference § 14 CFR 121.313 
paragraph (l). 
 
As stated in Final Rule “Installation and 
Operation of Flightdeck Installed 
Physical Secondary Barrier on 
Trasnposport Category Airplanes in 
Part 121 Service” paragraph IV, I 
Master Minimum Equipment List. This 
final rule states the FAA does not 
consider an IPSB” to be essential for 
safe operations under all operating 
conditions” and “the IPSB may be 
included in an operators MEL.” 
 
MMEL relief must be added into the 
A320 Familiy MMEL for an operator to 
develop approved MEL relief. 
 
Airlines for America (A4A) submitted 
MMEL PL-138 to the FAA in November 
2024 with a requested approval by 
March 2025. This PL has not yet been 
approved. Without knowing if the 
Global Change (GC) aspect of 
proposed MMEL PL-138 wil lbe 
approved, and not knowing when the 
FAA will approve PL-138, MMEL relief 
needs to be incorporated into the FAA 
A320 Familiy MMEL so operators 
might have a chance of having MEL 
relief by August 25, 2025. 
Recommend using existing FAA 
approved language from FAA 737-MAX 
MMEL Rev. 6 and will be used in FAA 787 
MMEL Rev. 19 (approved during 787 
FOEB meeting on 04/04/2025). Please 
note, this is the same language proposed 
to the FAA in November 2024 as FAA 
MMEL IPSB PL-138..
