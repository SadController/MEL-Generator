# Main conclusion

FAA MMEL Rev.32 dated 30.07.2025 is derived from the official FAA DRS and marked there Current. This is the main candidate for the subsequent formation of the base. GetJet MEL of 03.12.2021 - training archival copy only for listed A319/A320; A321 in its table boards are not. # File verification and verification limits

All 20 PDFs have signature, opening, number of pages and extraction of text from all pages; SHA-256 is calculated. All copies of the D:\A320 have been verified against the original SHA-256.
- Checked titles, letters of issue, history of revisions and/or lists of existing pages. Scanned WBM/SOP/NEF pages and FAA/TC titles are viewed visually.
WBM is predominantly scanned: text is only extracted from 9 out of 227 pages. A follow-up search inside will require an OCR.
Technical readability does not prove the completeness of the controlled set: line-by-line reconciliation of all sheets with LEP/LESS, verification of temporary revisions and AD for a specific board are not included in this stage. For FAA Rev.32, the presence of all ATA partitions from the table of contents and the absence of breaks in the internal numbering of system pages were checked.
Items of different operators and regulatory frameworks should not be mixed. Applicability to engines/modifications and Fenix is to be checked in step 3; applicability is not inferred from the general inscription A318/A319/A320/A321 on the cover.
Search index dates and PDF metadata are not equated with revision dates. For ACG and the Airbus manual, the exact version is not established.
Third-party copies are marked separately; they do not confirm the current status of MEL operator. There is no permission to distribute the source manuals as part of the public annex.
