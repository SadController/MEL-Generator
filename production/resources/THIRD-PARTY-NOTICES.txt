MEL Generator 1.0.0

Runtime: Electron 44.3.0 (MIT), including Chromium and Node.js.
Electron's LICENSE.electron.txt and LICENSES.chromium.html are distributed
alongside the installed application and in the Portable runtime extraction.
Build tool: electron-builder 26.15.3 (MIT). Build dependencies are listed in
package.json and pnpm-lock.yaml in the source archive.

Document: FAA A320 MMEL, Revision 32, July 30, 2025.
Source: https://drs.faa.gov/browse/excelExternalWindow/DRSDOCID152739505920250730144819.0001
The bundled PDF is an unchanged copy of the agreed source. English card text
is a summary of the selected provisions; it does not reproduce full (M)/(O)
procedures or constitute an operator MEL.

Airbus and Fenix names identify the simulated aircraft. This application is
an independent flight simulation project and is not affiliated with Airbus,
Fenix Simulations or the FAA.
